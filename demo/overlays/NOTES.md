# Farm Ajo v5 — AI overlay backgrounds

Generated 2026-11-10 at Dexter's request (voice note): faint farm-imagery
backgrounds so the demo video's screens stop sitting on plain backdrops.
Intended use: composite at low opacity (~15–25%) behind the demo page
screenshots — the imagery should be felt, not watched. All four are 16:9,
calm, and low-contrast enough to sit under UI. **No orange anywhere** —
palettes are green, brown, muted grey, and soft pale-gold light only
(brand rule). Each image was visually checked after generation.

| File | Size | What it shows | Intended scene |
|---|---|---|---|
| `overlay1_farmland.png` | 2048×1152 | Painterly aerial patchwork of green and brown fields in morning mist, soft gold horizon, no focal subject | **1 — Title / opening**: the world of the story before any UI appears |
| `overlay2_farmers.png` | 2048×1152 | Rolling green fields with smallholder farmers as tiny distant figures working the planting rows, hazy warm light | **2 — Circle / contributions**: the eight farmers and the pot filling up |
| `overlay3_bags.png` | 1920×1080 | Orderly rows of plain woven sacks stacked in a rural supply warehouse, soft window light, no text or logos on the bags | **3 — Purchase / delivery**: the bulk buy arriving; also suits the two-signature receipt beat |
| `overlay4_seedlings.png` | 1920×1080 | Cupped hands holding dark soil with a single small green seedling, deep-green bokeh background | **4 — Closing / end card**: the payoff — what the pot was for |

Notes for whoever composites v5:
- Overlays 3 and 4 were generated at 3:2 and center-cropped to 1920×1080
  (16:9) — no content of consequence was lost.
- Keep opacity low (15–25%) and consider a slight blur on overlay 4: the
  hands are the most recognisable subject of the four and could pull
  focus from the end-card text at higher opacity.
- Overlay 2's farmers are deliberately distant — at faint opacity it
  reads as texture, not people.
