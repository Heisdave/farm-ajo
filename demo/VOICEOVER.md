# Farm Ajo — demo video voiceover script (v2)

**Voice (Dexter's decision, 2026-11-10):** generated, not recorded.
Microsoft Edge TTS, Nigerian English — first choice
`en-NG-AbeoNeural` (male), fallback `en-NG-EzinneNeural`. Calm
delivery: rate **-10%**, neutral pitch. Generated per scene so each
line is fitted to its cut; no line may be cut off.

**Total: 142 words ≈ 60 s at that rate.** Plain and human. It opens
with the farmers line, names the verifier out loud (the founder's QC
credibility is said, not implied), and closes with the tagline.
